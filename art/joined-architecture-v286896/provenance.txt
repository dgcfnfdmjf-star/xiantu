Final architecture
source/architecture-majestic.png is the final image_gen edit of architecture-majestic-draft.png,
which was generated by editing architecture-grounded.png with north-terrain.png as material
reference. All architecture generations use transparent_background=true. User requested more
realistic, dimensional and imposing architecture: substantial tiled roof volumes and supporting
brackets, deep eaves and recessed galleries, round modeled timber columns, layered stone steps,
three-quarter side facades, coherent upper-left daylight, restrained warm metal and lanterns.
The second pass preserved all six designs and separated their source footprints for atlas use.
Original grounded and courtyard sources remain as earlier iterations, not runtime art.
Preserves six building types and the 3-column/2-row layout. Courtyard edges now dissolve into
broken buried paving, soil, moss and low vegetation. Removed the solid perimeter edging and
rectangular path tongues; integrated contact shadow instead of a detached oval shadow.
The runtime keeps a fixed world footprint instead of camera-relative depth scaling, with
matching picking/culling geometry and a stable central approach anchor.
